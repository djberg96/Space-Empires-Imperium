## Overall Goal

The goal is to mashup the old GDW Imperium board game with the more modern Space Empires 4x from GMT games.

## General Ideas

* Try to follow the original theme and any relevant fiction from the Traveller RPG.
* Stick with the point to point map. Ditch sublight movement.
* Replace original ships with best analogues from Space Empires 4x. Use ships from both the base
  Space Empires 4x game as well as the Close Encounters expansion.

## Specifics

* No aliens.
* Technology upgrades should probably be highly restricted given what I think the overall time
  of the conflict was supposed to take place in. Possibly only between wars.
* Restrict the technologies in general to things that make sense for this conflict.
* Definitely want the ground combat stuff!

## New Things

* A deck of cards with various events, bonuses, etc that players can use in limited amounts.
  Stuff that gives the game a little flavor but does not tilt the game. Perhaps the players
  get some for free but can purchase more.
