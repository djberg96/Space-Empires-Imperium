\section{THE IMPERIUM DECK}

The shared 42-card deck represents intelligence, political favors, operational
plans, and friction. Keep hands secret. The manifest below is authoritative;
card faces abbreviate these clarifications where space requires.

\subsection{Starting Hands}

At the start of a war, Terra draws one card per friendly controlled World. The
Imperium draws one card per two friendly controlled Worlds, rounded up. This is
that side's \textbf{minimum hand size} for the war. In \textit{First Shots},
Terra begins with three cards and the Imperium with two.

The hand limit is the number of connected friendly Worlds and Outposts. If a
player exceeds it, discard immediately to the limit. A minimum hand size never
forces a hand above its current limit.

\subsection{Card Step}

In the Draw, Discard, and Purchase Cards step, draw until reaching the minimum
hand size. Then a player may discard unwanted cards for 1 RU each and buy cards
for 5 RU each. Discards happen before purchases. A purchased card cannot be
discarded in the same Logistics Phase.

When the deck is empty, shuffle the face-up discard pile to form a new deck.
If a card instructs an earlier reshuffle, include that card unless stated
otherwise.

\subsection{Playing Cards}

A card can be played only at its listed timing. Each player may play at most
one card in a particular phase or combat substep. The attacker declares a card
first in combat; the First Player declares first at other shared timings. If
effects conflict, resolve the card played later after applying prohibitions.

After resolving a card, discard it face up. A player must satisfy every stated
requirement. Effects that choose ``ships'' choose individual ships even when a
numeral marker represents several ships in one group.

\subsection{Between Wars}

Cards do not carry into the next war. For each card left in hand, choose one:
gain 3 RU for interwar production, or prevent one ship's attrition roll. Two
cards are required to protect a BB or DN. Then discard all hands and calculate
new starting hands after deployment.

\subsection{Card Manifest}

\subsubsection{A Call to Arms (2)}

\textbfit{Combat --- start of the second firing round.} Ships from one adjacent
system may enter this battle. At least one of your original combat-capable
ships must have survived round one. Bases cannot move; Fighters require
Carrier capacity. The reinforcements did not move for purposes of later
reaction eligibility.

\subsubsection{Close the Range (2)}

\textbfit{Space Combat --- before the first firing round.} The first firing
round is at Close Range instead of Long Range. Resolve beam fire first, then
short-range missile fire with its normal penalties.

\subsubsection{Commandos (2)}

\textbfit{Planetary Operations --- when landing.} Move up to three Infantry
from one friendly surface in an adjacent system directly to the invaded
surface. They require no Transport and may fire in the first ground round.

\subsubsection{Confused Fighting (1)}

\textbfit{Space Combat --- before first-round screening.} Skip Range Control
for this battle. When each ship fires, treat the range as optimal for its
chosen weapon:
Beam and Missile Packages use full Attack, while a Mixed Package uses $-1$.
Resolve beam classes A--E and then missile classes A--E, regardless of the
current band. This affects both sides. After every combat in the current Combat
Phase is complete, shuffle the discard pile into the deck.

\subsubsection{Direct Hit (2)}

\textbfit{Space Combat --- after rolling.} Change one of your attack dice to a
natural 1. Then draw one card. The die must have been part of a legal attack.

\subsubsection{Economic Boom (2)}

\textbfit{Logistics --- after connections are determined.} Each connected
friendly Outpost produces one additional RU this turn. The Imperial player may
instead cancel a Depression result before its RU or card loss is applied.

\subsubsection{Electronic Warfare (2)}

\textbfit{Space Combat --- after groups are revealed.} Choose up to three enemy
groups. In the first firing round, each chosen group fires one class later
(Class E remains E).

\subsubsection{Escape Plan (1)}

\textbfit{Combat --- before a firing round.} Retreat every one of your ships
from the battle to the same legal adjacent system without enemy fire. If you
retreat from a friendly World, adjust Glory one point in the opponent's favor:
$-1$ for an Imperial retreat or $+1$ for a Terran retreat.

\subsubsection{Flank Speed (2)}

\textbfit{Reaction Movement.} One reacting stack may make three more jumps
than its current Reaction Allowance, to a maximum of six. Refuelling and enemy
interception still apply.

\subsubsection{Intel (2)}

\textbfit{Any time except combat.} Look at the opponent's hand, choose one card,
and discard it.

\subsubsection{Logistics (2)}

\textbfit{Logistics --- before maintenance.} Gain 5 RU. One friendly stack is
treated as supplied for maintenance and movement throughout this Game Turn.

\subsubsection{Mass Drivers (2)}

\textbfit{Bombardment.} Before normal bombardment, make one bonus Attack 7
against the surface. Apply its Defense but no ship technology or Fleet Size.
On a hit, remove one Militia.

\subsubsection{Minefield (2)}

\textbfit{Space Combat --- before defensive reactions.} Play only in a system
containing a friendly World or Outpost. Randomly select two enemy ships, or
every enemy ship if fewer than two are present. After reactions are declared,
make a separate Missile Attack 5 against each, subtracting its normal Defense,
Defense Tech, and Point Defense. Each hit causes one damage.

\subsubsection{Overrun (1)}

\textbfit{Immediately after Space Combat.} If every enemy ship was destroyed
or retreated, one victorious stack may make one jump. Resolve any new battle
immediately. The stack cannot refuel during this jump and Fleet Trains cannot
take part.

\subsubsection{Queued Up (2)}

\textbfit{Reaction Movement.} Move up to three reacting stacks instead of one.
Each uses the normal Reaction Allowance and all usual restrictions.

\subsubsection{Reckless Attack (2)}

\textbfit{Combat --- before one firing round.} As each of your ships fires, you
may give it +2 Attack for that shot. Until the end of the round, enemy attacks
against that ship receive +1 Attack. Mark participating ships as necessary.

\subsubsection{Resistance (2)}

\textbfit{Ground Combat --- before the first round.} Every defending permanent
Ground Unit receives +1 Attack and +1 Defense in the first two rounds. Militia
receive +1 Attack only.

\subsubsection{Sabotage (1)}

\textbfit{Immediately after enemy production.} Choose an enemy ship group in a
system containing an enemy Outpost adjacent to one of your Worlds or Outposts.
Roll a d10; on 1--7, the group suffers one hit allocated by its owner. Then
shuffle the entire discard pile, including this card, into the deck.

\subsubsection{Snafu (2)}

\textbfit{Start of an enemy Player Turn.} Choose one system containing an enemy
Outpost. Enemy stacks that begin a normal Movement Phase there may make no more
than two jumps in that phase.

\subsubsection{Standoff (2)}

\textbfit{Space Combat --- before the first firing round.} If both sides have a
combat-capable ship after round one, combat ends. The attacker retreats all
ships first, then the defender; no retreat fire occurs. A ship with no legal
destination remains in the system.

\subsubsection{Supply Run (2)}

\textbfit{Start of your Movement Phase.} One stack ignores its supply movement
penalty and need not refuel for its first three jumps this phase. Enemy ships
still stop it.

\subsubsection{Surprise (2)}

\textbfit{Space Combat --- before the first firing round.} Choose Long or Close
Range for each of the first two firing rounds. Skip Range Control before round
two. If opposing range-setting cards conflict, the defender chooses the range
for round one and the attacker chooses it for round two.

\subsubsection{Tactics (2)}

\textbfit{Space Combat --- before the first firing round.} Treat your Tactics
as one level higher for Range Control and firing-order ties in this battle.
Once in each firing round, reroll one of your attack dice; the new result is
final.
